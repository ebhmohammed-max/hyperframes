OLD VALYRIA — DOCUMENTARY STYLE SAMPLER
======================================

What it is
  Eight 90-second treatments of the *planned* Old Valyria episode content, so you can
  choose a visual language before committing the full film. Same selected narrator
  (voice-01) introduces each approach; the following 60 seconds reuse exact excerpts of
  the existing 35-minute narration, with music, designed sound effects and on-screen
  technique labels.

The eight treatments (sampler clock)
  00:00  Cinematic history            - scale, sustained shots, restrained title
  01:30  Archival essay               - reading cards, source and uncertainty labels
  03:00  Investigative history        - competing dragon-origin claims, open questions
  04:30  Geographic explainer         - schematic peninsula, route reveal, volcanoes
  06:00  Natural-history explainer    - simplified anatomy silhouette and detail cutaways
  07:30  Political history            - conceptual house network, power without one throne
  09:00  Human-cost documentary       - mines, slower movement, narration-first sound
  10:30  Reflective essay             - juxtaposition, thematic words, closing question

How to judge it
  1. Which two or three treatments suit Old Valyria best?
  2. Where should graphics replace imagery, and where should they stay out of the way?
  3. Is the movement distracting or too restrained?
  4. Is the narration / music / sound-effect balance right?
  5. Should the final episode feel like cinematic history, an evidence-led essay, or a blend?

What this is NOT
  No authentic interviews, expert testimony, recovered footage, scanned manuscripts or
  measured data are shown or implied. Reading cards are designed for this sampler, maps
  are schematic, the anatomy is simplified, and the political network is illustrative,
  not a complete genealogy. All illustrations are AI-generated; narration is synthetic.
  This is an unofficial fan documentary demonstration, not a new lore source.
