# EP14 Bhangarh: fact sheet

Written 18 Nov 2026 with script v1.1. The film keeps three things apart and labels them on screen: what records say, what local people tell, and what cannot be shown at all.

**How far the checking went.** Every source below was read as a search result or a short extract, not as a full page or a book. No inscription, gazetteer or Archaeological Survey (ASI) publication was read first-hand. The notice board was read from a small web photograph. Everything marked **check** should be read against a primary source before the final voice recording.

On-screen tags: **வரலாற்றுப் பதிவு** (record) · **ஊர்க் கதை** (local story) · **தொல்லியல் துறை** (ASI) · **ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து** (what researchers say) · **நிரூபிக்கப்படாத கூற்று** (unproven claim).

## 2. The two legends (clips N03, N04): local tradition, not record

| Legend as told in the script | Notes |
|---|---|
| Guru Balu Nath allowed the town on condition that the palace shadow never fall on his place; Ajab Singh raised the palace; the curse emptied the town and no roof will stand | Told with many differences. | Princess Ratnavati, 18; the sorcerer Singhia enchants perfumed oil bought by her maid; she pours it on a boulder, which rolls and crushes him; his dying curse; war with Ajabgarh the next year | Versions differ (in some the bottle itself turns into the boulder; some date it about 1720). Not in any record seen: a princess Ratnavati of Bhangarh. One genealogy site (rajputs.net) lists a "Rajkumari Ratnawati Kanwar" as a daughter of Chhatr Singh; no other source. The script says only that no firm old record of the story has been shown.

## 4. The notice board (clips N01, N06)

Read from a small web photograph of the board (tripoto.com and quora.com copies of the same board). Hindi, as read:

> भारत सरकार · भारतीय पुरातत्व सर्वेक्षण, भानगढ़
> आवश्यक सूचना:
> (1) भानगढ़ की सीमा में सूर्योदय से पहले एवं सूर्यास्त के पश्चात प्रवेश वर्जित है।
> (2) भानगढ़ की सीमा में किसी भी प्रकार के मवेशियों का प्रवेश कराना कानूनन अपराध है, आज्ञा का उल्लंघन करने पर कानूनी कार्यवाही की जावेगी।
> (3) भानगढ़ की सीमा में केवड़े के वृक्ष भारतीय पुरातत्व सर्वेक्षण विभाग (भारत सरकार) की सम्पत्ति है। केवड़े के वृक्षों को किसी भी प्रकार की क्षति पहुँचाना मना है।
> नोट: उपरोक्त आदेशों का उल्लंघन करने पर कानूनी कार्यवाही की जावेगी।
> आज्ञा से — अधीक्षक पुरातत्वविद्

In English: (1) entry into the limits of Bhangarh before sunrise and after sunset is prohibited; (2) bringing cattle of any kind inside is an offence in law; (3) the kewda (screw-pine) trees inside belong to the ASI and must not be harmed; note: breaking these orders will bring legal action. By order, Superintending Archaeologist.
