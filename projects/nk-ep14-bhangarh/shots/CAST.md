Rules
- No ghost or apparition in any picture.
- Guru Balu Nath is honoured at a shrine on the site to this day: never by face (from behind, far away, or a hand). Always the same description: grey matted hair coiled on the head, long grey beard, ochre robe.
- Real rulers (Bhagwant Das, Man Singh, Madho Singh, Akbar, Chhatr Singh, Ajab Singh, Jai Singh) never by face.
- No blood, no bodies, nobody shown starving. Present-day people from behind or hands only.
- The board's lettering is never readable in a picture. Its real wording (three rules, in Hindi) is in FACT_SHEET.md and is set as text in the edit.
- Web photographs of the real site (`google_drive/ep14_pics/webref/`) were looked at only to learn what the places look like. They are not used in the film and were not given to the picture tool.
