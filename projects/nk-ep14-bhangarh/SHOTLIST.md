# EP14 Bhangarh: shot list

185 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration, not a photograph of the site. The views of the ruins follow the real layout (gateway, bazaar street, temples, palace on the slope) loosely, with reference pictures (`gate`, `bazaar`, `palace`, `temple`, `board`) to keep them alike. The legend figures Ratnavati and Singhia have invented faces, kept alike with reference pictures. Guru Balu Nath is honoured at a shrine on the site to this day, so he is never shown by face: from behind, far away or as hands. Real rulers (Bhagwant Das, Man Singh, Madho Singh, Akbar, Chhatr Singh, Ajab Singh, Jai Singh) are never shown by face: from behind, far away or as hands. No ghost or apparition is ever shown. No blood, no bodies, nobody shown starving. Present-day people from behind or as hands only. No readable writing: the notice board's lettering stays unreadable (its real wording is in FACT_SHEET.md and goes on screen in the edit).

Looks: **B** Bhangarh today, daylight; **N** Bhangarh after dark; **P** the town in its living days; **S** the legends (storybook mood); **M** records; **F** the famine of 1783; **W** wildlife of the Aravallis; **T** visitors and guards today; **X** symbolic still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
