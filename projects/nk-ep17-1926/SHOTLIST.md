# EP17 1926, an ordinary day: shot list

128 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration of how a day in Madras in 1926 may have looked, not a photograph. The family (father, mother, girl, a younger boy, a grandmother) is invented and the film says so in its last chapter; three of them are kept alike with reference pictures. No blood, no bodies, nobody shown being hurt. Illness and the death of infants are shown only through a hand on a forehead, an empty cloth cradle and unlit lamps; no sick or dead child is shown. Child marriage is shown only as a still life (a slate beside a folded silk sari) and a wedding house seen from far. Real people (Dr Muthulakshmi Reddy, J. L. Baird) are seen only from behind; the king's head on the coins is worn smooth. No readable writing: newspapers, tickets, postcards, telegrams and film title cards are set in the edit as mock-ups, and maps are drawn in the edit from real coastline data. Present-day people from behind or as hands only. Lines that carry a full-screen graphic have no picture.

Looks: **H** inside the house; **S** street and town, 1926; **W** work, 1926; **C** children and school, 1926; **E** cinema, stage and wireless, 1926; **N** night in the house; **T** present day; **X** still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
