Reference pictures go in `out/cast/` (stored in `<repo>/google_drive/ep18_pics/cast/`, Drive: `images/EP18_cast.zip`). They are passed as reference images so that the same tower, the same door and the same figure return through the film.

Rules
- No photograph of the inside of the vaults or of the treasure has been published. Every picture of them is an illustration built from the published descriptions (mainly the observers' accounts in the New Yorker, 2012). The film says so on screen where it matters.
- The iron door: nobody outside the 2011 team has described it in detail. The reference picture is an invention that follows the one known description ("an iron door, three latches, one jammed"). The carved cobra door that circulates online is shown only in the legend look and is called unconfirmed in the narration (N06).
- The god: never shown clearly. Inside the temple, at most an indistinct dark shape behind lamps. The small golden figure from Vault A is shown wrapped in cloth.
- Real, named or identifiable people: the petitioner only from behind (he died in July 2011; no face, no portrait). Observers, the auditor, judges, lawyers, priests, committee members and the royal family from behind, as hands, or not at all.
- The petitioner's death (N06 q1): an empty cane chair and a lamp at a doorstep. No body, no funeral.
- Legends in look **L** only (serpent, spell, sea behind the door, sage and eagle, the "imagine" pictures of N07 q9), so that they cannot be mistaken for record.
- No readable writing in pictures. The map, the vault plan, the three-door counter and the audit page are built in the edit (`film/fx.mjs`).
- Police and firemen: generic uniforms, no badges, no lettering.

Rows that use a reference: marked `@temple`, `@irondoor`, `@petitioner` in `shots/shots.txt` (29 rows).
