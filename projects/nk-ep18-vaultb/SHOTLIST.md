# EP18 Padmanabhaswamy temple, Vault B: shot list

127 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: no photograph of the vaults or of the treasure has been published, so every picture is an illustration of what the published accounts describe, not a record. The image of the god is never shown clearly: at most an indistinct dark shape behind lamps, and the small golden figure from the vault is shown wrapped in cloth. Real people (the petitioner T. P. Sundararajan, the observers, the auditor, judges, lawyers, priests, the royal family) are seen only from behind, as hands, or not at all; nobody's face is shown. The petitioner's death is shown only through an empty chair and a lamp at a doorstep. Legends (the serpent, the spell, the sea behind the door, the sage and the eagle) are painted in their own dream-like look so that they cannot be taken for record. The iron door is always plain, as the 2011 observers described it. No readable writing: reports, registers and newspapers are blurred lines; the map, the vault plan and the report page are built in the edit. Lines that carry a full-screen graphic have no picture.

Looks: **T** the temple town, 2011 to today; **V** under the temple: corridor and vault; **G** the treasure; **D** inside the temple; **K** Travancore, 18th century; **S** the pepper trade, ancient times; **C** courts and offices; **L** a legend, painted as a legend; **X** still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
