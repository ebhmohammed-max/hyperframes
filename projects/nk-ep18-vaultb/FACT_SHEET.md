# EP18 fact sheet: Padmanabhaswamy temple, Vault B

Film: *பத்மநாபசுவாமி கோயில்: திறக்காத கதவு (B நிலவறை)*, about 9 minutes. Script: `SCRIPT_TA.md` (v2, 20 Nov 2026).

**How this was checked.** Nine web searches on 20 Nov 2026. The sources below were read as search-result extracts, not as full pages, so quotations are short and page-level details (exact wording, page numbers) still need a reader with the full text. Every claim in the film carries one of six labels on screen: நீதிமன்ற பதிவு (court record), செய்தி அறிக்கை (press report), வரலாற்றுப் பதிவு (historical record), கோயில் மரபு (temple tradition), நம்பிக்கை · கதை (belief, legend), கருத்து (opinion).

**Care points.** This is a living temple and several people in the story are alive. Named people appear only where a court or a published report names them. The petitioner's death is told as a fact (fever, age 70) and the "curse" as what people said. The audit's findings are given as the audit's, followed at once by the royal family's reply. No photograph of the vaults or the treasure has been published, so all pictures are illustrations; the image of the god is never shown clearly.

## Doubts to settle before upload

1. **27 or 30 June 2011.** The inventory began on 27 June (WK, WP); Vaults A and B were approached on 30 June (NY). The film says "30 June" for the slab and "the last week of June" for the start. Both fit, but a reader with the full NY article should confirm 30 June.
2. **The value.** ₹90,000 crore, ₹1 lakh crore, ₹1.2 lakh crore and 20 to 22 billion dollars all appear in print. None is official; the court-ordered inventory has not been published. The film says so twice.
3. **"Seven-member team."** Wikipedia gives seven; NY names five kinds of members without a number. Recorded in the narration (N03 q5). If wrong, the fix is a caption note or a re-recorded line.
4. **"Many keys."** The narration (N04 q0) says the grille door was opened "with many keys". No source checked says how it was opened. Safer wording: "அதை திறக்கறாங்க".
5. **The cobra story: 1908 or 1931.** The 1933 book speaks of an attempt "about 25 years ago". A vault or antechamber was also opened in December 1931 on the Maharaja's orders (*The Hindu*, 1931). Later articles mix the two. The film keeps to what the book says and gives no year for the event.
6. **Seven openings or the antechamber only.** The audit read the registers as openings of Vault B. The royal family says those entries mean the outer room, and that the inner chamber behind the iron door has never been opened. The film gives both and does not decide.
7. **Idol reconsecration, 1731 or 1739.** Sources differ. The film does not give a year.
8. **The audit's other findings** (769 gold pots said to be missing, about 776 kg; NN, 2016) are not in the film: they are contested and would need the other side at length.
9. **Titles and Tamil wording** are drafts for a native review, as for every episode.
