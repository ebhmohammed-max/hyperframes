# EP20 picture rules

No reference pictures: nobody returns through the film. (`out/cast/` stays empty.)

Rules
- Every picture is an illustration. None is a photograph or a record, and none shows the real temple exactly.
- Three looks keep the claims apart: **L** the legend as it is told (golden, dreamlike: the long ramp, the single stone); **C** a sober reconstruction of 1010 (what scholars suggest; not a record); **T** the temple today. **H** is Tamil Nadu today away from the temple, **D** still lifes, **O** has its setting in the subject.
- The king only from behind. Workers, scholars and visitors are invented; no real, living or identifiable person. Faces turned away or small; children from behind.
- No close view of any deity; no view into the sanctum.
- Inscriptions appear only as worn, illegible chisel marks. Books and pages have blurred print. No readable writing, logos, flags or emblems; every number, name and source is set in the edit (`film/fx.mjs`).
- Women of 1010 in plain draped saris, dancers far away and from behind.
