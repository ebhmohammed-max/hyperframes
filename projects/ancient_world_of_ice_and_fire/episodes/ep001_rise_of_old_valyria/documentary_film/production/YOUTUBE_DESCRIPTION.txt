RISE OF OLD VALYRIA — From Shepherds to Dragonlords
The Long Night | Episode 01

How did a people remembered as shepherds become dragonlords—and who paid for the world they built?

This illustrated fan documentary follows Valyria's beginnings: the volcanic peninsula beneath the Fourteen Flames, the mystery of dragon origins and mastery, the rise of the ruling houses, and the human cost behind their power. It ends at the threshold of the five wars against Old Ghis.

The surviving accounts within the fictional world do not answer every question. Traditions, disputed origins and imagined reconstructions are distinguished from established claims in the narration. Scenes of daily life and unnamed people are illustrative, not recovered eyewitness records.
