# Art defects — open and closed

Masters are generated blind against a written direction. A model can satisfy the
words and still produce something impossible, and a thumbnail contact sheet will
hide it. These are the ones found so far.

## OPEN

### 032 — modern office in antiquity

**Wrong:** electric oscillating desk fan, ballpoint pen, wristwatch, short-sleeved
shirt with a machine collar. This is a mid-20th-century office.

**Why it happened:** the direction asked for "a cramped administrator's office in
a hot foreign city, an exhausted man writing at a small desk". "Office" and
"ledgers" pulled the model toward the modern world. Nothing in the prompt
forbade modern objects.

**Fix:** regenerate with an explicit ban — clay tablets and reed stylus, no
electric anything, no modern clothing, no glass windows. Replacement must pass a
full-size look before it goes into the plate set.

**Blast radius:** 15 plates (0436–0450). They already contain the fan. They are
not in the film yet.

## CLOSED

### 034 — suspected modern market, checked, clean

Suspicion raised from the contact sheet. At full size the market is entirely
period: baskets, amphorae, a bronze balance scale, no modern objects. Kept.

## Rule going forward

Every master gets a full-size look before its plates are trusted. The contact
sheet catches composition; it does not catch a desk fan.
