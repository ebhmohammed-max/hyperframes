## 1. The last day (label U, times label P)

Source read in full: Srijan Pal Singh's Facebook note of 28 July 2015, "What I will be remembered for", as printed by Governance Now (https://www.governancenow.com/news/regular-story/-facebook-kalams-aide-srijan-pal-singh-recalls-his-last-day); also carried by The Quint and Oneindia.

| Script says | Source says |
|---|---|

| Turbulence; Kalam pulls down the window shade: "now you don't see any fear" | Singh writes this as a habit ("Whenever he would see me go cold in shaking plane"), on a flight of 2.5 hours "in the monsoon weather". The script tells it as a moment of this flight. **Small liberty.** |

| Radio message did not get through; he reminded three times; "I want to meet him and thank him" | "We tried radio messaging, that did not work. For the next 1.5 hours of the journey, he reminded me thrice"; quote as given. |

Times (label P): collapsed about 6:35 pm IST; taken to Bethany Hospital, Shillong; declared dead of cardiac arrest at 7:45 pm; aged 83 (born 15 Oct 1931). From press summaries (search results, several pages agreeing). **Singh writes "two minutes into the speech"; press reports say about five minutes.** The script gives no figure.

## 2. Childhood and the lost place (label S)

From `Wings of Fire` (1999) as quoted on secondary pages:

- Born 1931, Rameswaram; his father Jainulabdeen had a boat (ferrying pilgrims). *The boat is from general knowledge of the book, not from a page seen in this session.*
- Second World War: the train's halt at Rameswaram was suspended, newspaper bundles were thrown from the moving train on the Rameswaram–Dhanushkodi line; his cousin Samsuddin, the distributor, had him catch them: "my first wages", remembered with pride half a century later.
- Aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology (general knowledge, not checked here).
- Air Force Selection Board, Dehradun: ninth in a batch of 25; eight were commissioned. Year 1958 on most pages, 1957 on one; the script gives no year.
- Rishikesh, Swami Sivananda: "Accept your destiny and go ahead with your life. You are not destined to become an Air Force pilot. What you are destined to become is not revealed now but it is predetermined."
- Then Senior Scientific Assistant at the Directorate of Technical Development and Production (Air), Delhi: design work on aircraft.

## 3. SLV-3 (dates label F, the story label S)

- 10 Aug 1979, Sriharikota, first SLV-3 launch, Kalam project director: failed, fell into the Bay of Bengal. 18 July 1980: Rohini satellite placed in orbit. (Public record.)
- The story of the computer hold, the experts, his decision, the spin in the second stage, and Satish Dhawan taking the press conference ("we have failed today… I want to support my technologists… next year they succeed"), then sending Kalam to the press after the success: all from Kalam's own talks as reported by the press (DNA, The Hindu and others, 2013).
- **Versions differ on when the computer stopped the count** (some say 40 seconds before launch, some say minutes). The script says "in the last minutes".
- "Many years of work, thousands of people": his talks say thousands; reports of the number of years vary (7 to 10), so the script gives no number.
- `Frontline` (18 Jan 2002) prints a longer, different wording of Dhawan's statement. The film's wording is Kalam's recollection and is labelled as his telling.
- "35 years later": 1980 to 2015.

## 4. The 52 relatives (label U)

P. M. Nair (his secretary as President), `The Kalam Effect`: May 2006, 52 relatives, eight days at Rashtrapati Bhavan; no official car used; he paid for rooms and food himself, Rs 3.52 lakh. One review page says 2.5 lakh; the book text found online says 3.52. Book text seen only through an online copy of unknown standing.

## 5. What he left (label P)

Press reports of August 2015 (DailyO, 4 Aug 2015, repeated widely): 2,500 books, a wristwatch, six shirts, four trousers, three suits, one pair of shoes; no house of his own, no car, TV, fridge or air conditioner. **This is a press list, not an official inventory**; its first source is not clear, and the same article has at least one plain error elsewhere. The film says "the list the press reports give" and labels it P.

"More than 40 years of government service": 1958 to 1999 (DRDO, ISRO, DRDO, Principal Scientific Adviser), then President 2002–2007.

## 6. Rameswaram (label P)

Laid to rest 30 July 2015 at Pei Karumbu, Rameswaram, with state honours. Crowd: "over one lakh" (ANI, the same day) to "over 350,000" (later pages). The script says "more than a lakh".

## To check before publishing

1. `Wings of Fire`: the newspaper bundles, "first wages", ninth of 25, Sivananda's words: confirm against the book.
2. `The Kalam Effect`: 52 relatives, eight days, Rs 3.52 lakh: confirm against the book.
3. The possessions list: find its first source, or keep the "press reports" wording exactly as it is.
4. The turbulence moment is told as part of this flight; the note gives it as a habit. Change one word in the script if this matters ("விமானம் குலுங்கும்போதெல்லாம்").
5. Tamil spellings of names: ஸ்ரீஜன் பால் சிங், சதீஷ் தவான், சிவானந்தா, கௌஹாத்தி, ஷில்லாங்.
6. The film shows no face of Dr Kalam (pictures are generated illustrations). If real photographs are wanted, they need licences.
