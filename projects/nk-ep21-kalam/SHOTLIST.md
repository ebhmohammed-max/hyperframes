# EP21 அப்துல் கலாமின் கடைசி நாள் (Abdul Kalam's last day, 27 July 2015): shot list

100 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration, none is a photograph or a record. **No face of a real person is shown.** Dr Kalam appears only from behind, in silhouette, from far, or as hands (silver hair to the collar, dark closed-collar suit); the same holds for him as a boy, as a young man and as project director, and for his aide, Satish Dhawan and Swami Sivananda. The guard's face is covered, as the aide's account describes him. The collapse, the hospital room and the burial are not shown: an empty podium, a corridor and a crowd seen from far stand for them. Three looks keep the times apart: **D** the last day (monsoon, cool), **R** Rameswaram around 1940 (sepia), **Y** 1958 to 1980 (faded colour). No readable writing, logos, flags or emblems; every number, name and source is set in the edit (film/fx.mjs).

Looks: **D** the last day, 27 July 2015; **P** the last day, on the flight; **R** Rameswaram around 1940, as he remembered it; **Y** 1958 to 1980, the working years; **S** symbolic still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
