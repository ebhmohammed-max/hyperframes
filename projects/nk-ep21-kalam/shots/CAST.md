# EP21 picture rules

No reference pictures. People are held together by fixed descriptions in `shots/expand.py` (`REF`).

- Every picture is an illustration made for this film. None is a photograph or a record.
- **No face of a real person.** Dr Kalam (K) is shown only from behind, in silhouette, from far, or as hands: silver hair to the collar, parted in the middle; a dark closed-collar suit. The same rule for him as a boy (B), a young man (Y) and project director (M), for his aide (A), Satish Dhawan and Swami Sivananda.
- The guard (G) is covered in black cloth with only the eyes showing, as the aide's note describes him. He is not a likeness of anyone.
- Not shown: the fall, the hospital room, the body, the burial. An empty podium, a corridor and a crowd seen from far stand for them.
- Three looks keep the times apart: **D** 27 July 2015 (monsoon, cool); **R** Rameswaram around 1940 (sepia); **Y** 1958 to 1980 (faded colour). **S** still lifes, **O** setting in the subject.
- No readable writing, logos, flags, emblems or medals; clocks have no numerals. Every number, name and source is set in the edit (`film/fx.mjs`).
- Real buildings (Rashtrapati Bhavan, the old Parliament House, the Pamban bridge) appear only from far and are not exact.
