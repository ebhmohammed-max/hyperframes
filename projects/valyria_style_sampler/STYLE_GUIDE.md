## Recommended direction for the full Valyria episode
Use cinematic history as the connective language; investigative reading cards for genuinely disputed origins; maps only when they clarify space; diagrams only when they clarify a relationship; and restrained human-cost sequences in the mining chapter. The sampler exaggerates the distinctions to make comparison easier. A finished episode should not switch visual language merely to show off an effect.

## Implemented in this sampler
- Eight editable HyperFrames compositions and a720second root timeline.
- Original narrative excerpts and eight new selected-voice introductions.
- Camera push/drift, still-image cutaways, chapter cards, source/uncertainty labels.
- Designed reading cards; **not** scans, facsimiles or authenticated manuscripts.
- Schematic SVG geography and conceptual route reveal; **not** a geographic reference map.
- Simplified fictional-anatomy silhouette; **not** a biological/engineering simulation.
- Six-node political network as an illustration; **not** all forty families, a census, ranking or verified genealogy.
- Narration ducking, musical fades and restrained CC0 chapter punctuation.
- Source hashes, credits, provenance, technical checks and editable source packaging.

## Not demonstrated / not claimed
Real interviews or expert testimony; live-action field production; authentic archival footage; new lore/book fact-checking; measured statistical charts; photoreal3D simulation; generated moving dragon footage; an avatar presenter; speaker diarization; word-aligned subtitles; multilingual narration; platform monetization approval; full image/franchise publication-rights clearance. Some of these can be separate workflows when suitable tools, source material, permissions and review are available. They have not been silently substituted into this video.
