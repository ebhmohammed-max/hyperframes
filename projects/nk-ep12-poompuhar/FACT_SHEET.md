# EP12 பூம்புகார்: fact sheet

For script v1 (16 Nov 2026). **Tag** is the label shown on screen:
I இலக்கியம் (what a text says) · A அகழாய்வு (dug on land) · S கடல் ஆய்வு (found or measured at sea) · O ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து (a scholar's reading) · C நிரூபிக்கப்படாத கூற்று (a claim with no dated material behind it).

## How far to trust this sheet

- The research was news reports, institute summary pages and one paper abstract, found by web search on 16 Nov 2026.
- I did not read the primary works: the ASI memoir (Soundararajan 1994), the Puratattva paper (Gaur and Sundaresh 1998), Rao 1991, Hancock's book, or the Tamil texts. What they say reaches the script through the secondary sources below.
- My notes record sources per topic, not per sentence. So the "Sources" line under each section covers the section as a whole. Where the notes do tie a claim to a source, the table says so.
- The topic bank asks for three sources per fact. **That bar is met only for the rows marked "3+".** Everything else is marked with what I have.
- **check** = verify before publishing. There are 24 rows with one.
- No native Tamil speaker and no archaeologist has read the script.
