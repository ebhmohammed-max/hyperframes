# EP12 பூம்புகார்: fact sheet

Status of each claim in script v1 (16 Nov 2026). **Tag** is the label shown on screen:
I இலக்கியம் (what a text says) · A அகழாய்வு (dug on land) · S கடல் ஆய்வு (found or measured at sea) · O ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து (a scholar's reading) · C நிரூபிக்கப்படாத கூற்று (a claim with no dated material behind it).

"Solid" means at least three independent sources agree. "Two sources" and "one source" are marked as such. **check** marks the items worth a second look before publishing.

How this was researched: news reports, institute pages and paper abstracts found by web search. I could not open the ASI memoir (Soundararajan 1994), the Puratattva paper (Gaur and Sundaresh 1998) or the Tamil texts themselves; what they say reaches the script through the secondary sources listed at the end. The script has not been read by a native Tamil speaker or checked by an archaeologist.

## Things the script leaves out on purpose

- "Destroyed around 300 BC" or "AD 500": web pages give these without a source.
- Kumari Kandam: a separate topic (M6 in the topic bank).
- The Minister's dive in September 2026: true, but not needed.
- The underwater excavation licence from the Centre: true (Indian Express), cut for length.
