# EP16 Sigiriya: shot list

144 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration, not a photograph of Sigiriya. No likeness of Kashyapa, Dhatusena, Moggallana or Migara has come down to us, so their faces are invented (the film says so in its last chapter) and kept alike with reference pictures. No blood, no bodies, nobody shown being hurt or killed: the old king's death is a newly bricked wall, the battle is dust and far figures, Kashyapa's death is a dagger and a crown in the grass. The painted women are drawn in the manner of the rock paintings but clothed in a blouse, which the originals are not; no surviving painting is copied. Buddhist monks are shown from behind and with respect; no Buddha image is shown. The legend pictures (look N) carry the tag for belief or claim on screen. Later scholars and officers (Forbes, Bell, Paranavitana, Raja de Silva, the 1967 conservator) from behind only. No readable writing; the scratched verses are always out of focus. Present-day people from behind or as hands only.

Looks: **A** Anuradhapura court, 5th century; **K** Sigiriya in Kashyapa's time, about 480 CE; **B** the battle of 495, shown without harm; **M** monks, always from behind; **P** the rock paintings; **N** legend, not record; **T** Sigiriya today; **V** 19th and 20th century, old photograph look; **X** symbolic still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
