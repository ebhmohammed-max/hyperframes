# EP16 reference pictures (invented; none is a portrait of a real person)

Reference pictures go in `out/cast/` (stored in `<repo>/google_drive/ep16_pics/cast/`, Drive: `images/EP16_cast.zip`). Pass them as reference images so the faces stay the same.

No statue, coin or painting showing Kashyapa or anyone in his family is known. Every face below is imagined, and the film says so in its last chapter. Dress follows the usual reading of 5th century Sri Lankan court dress (bare chest, waist cloth, heavy ornaments, hair in a topknot); it is a guess.

| Key | Who | Look |
|---|---|---|
| kashyapa | காசியப்பன், king 477–495, about 35 | lean face, sharp watchful eyes, short black beard and moustache, topknot, tall gold crown, heavy gold necklaces and armlets, deep red silk waist cloth |
| dhatusena | தாதுசேனன், his father, about 65 | lined face, white beard, white hair in a topknot, one gold necklace, white waist cloth with a gold border |
| moggallana | மொகல்லானன், his half-brother, about 20 | open face, clean-shaven, topknot, thin gold circlet, single gold chain, deep blue waist cloth |
| migara | மிகாரன், army commander, about 45 | heavy square face, thick black moustache, no beard, hair tied back, crossed leather chest straps with bronze discs, sword, dark green waist cloth |

For other ages say the age in the prompt: Kashyapa in 495 "in his fifties with grey in his beard"; Moggallana in 495 "about forty with a short beard"; Kashyapa before the coup "about thirty, no crown".

Rules
- No blood, no bodies, nobody shown being hurt or killed.
- When two reference faces share a picture, state each man's age and beard.
- Monks from behind, no faces; no Buddha image.
- The painted women are clothed (fitted blouse); no surviving painting is copied.
- No readable writing; scratched verses and manuscripts always out of focus.
- Later scholars and present-day people from behind or as hands only.
