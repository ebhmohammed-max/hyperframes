# EP11 ஆதித்த கரிகாலன்: fact sheet

Status of each claim in script v1. **Tag** is the label shown on screen: K கல்வெட்டு (inscription), C செப்பேடு (copper plate), A ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து (a scholar's reading), N நாவல் (the novel), U ஊகம் (guess).
"Solid" means the record itself says it. "Reading" means a named scholar argues it. "Disputed" means sources disagree and the script says so.

This sheet was written from the sources read during research (listed at the end). I could not open the Epigraphia Indica volume or *The Cōḷas* directly; the quotations from them come through the secondary sources named. Items marked **check** are the ones worth a second look before publishing.

## 4. The readings (all tagged A)

| Scholar | Reading as stated in the script | Source |
|---|---|---|
| K. A. Nilakanta Sastri (*The Cōḷas*, 1935–37) | Nothing was done in the years Uttama ruled; he gained the throne; "It seems impossible to acquit Uttama Cola of a part in the conspiracy" | quoted in Balasubramanian and elsewhere |
| Sastri, earlier | "Took the head" might be a figure of speech for a defeat | noted by Nagaswamy on the Esalam plates |
| T. V. Sadasiva Pandarathar (பிற்கால சோழர் சரித்திரம், 1949) | Had Uttama been guilty, Arulmozhi would not have yielded and the reign would not have been peaceful; "பஞ்சவன் பிரம்மாதிராஜன்" is a Pandya office, so this was revenge for Vira Pandya | Balasubramanian's survey |
| R. V. Srinivasan (1971) | Rajaraja and Kundavai were behind it; no evidence has been found | Balasubramanian's survey. **check** the place of publication if you name it; the script names only the year |

## 6. What the film does not claim

- It does not say who ordered the killing. The last chapter separates what the stone says (he was killed; three named brothers; property taken) from motive, which is tagged ஊகம்.
- It does not show or describe the killing. Pictures are reconstruction art; people are seen from behind or as silhouettes.
- It names no place, weapon or day for the death.
