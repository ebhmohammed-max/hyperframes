# EP11 cast (invented faces for the film; no portrait of any of these people survives)

Reference pictures are in `out/cast/` (Drive: `images/EP11_cast.zip`). Pass them as reference images so the faces stay the same.

| Key | Who | Look |
|---|---|---|
| aditya | ஆதித்த கரிகாலன், crown prince, about 25 | fierce eyes, thick moustache, high topknot with gold band, scar across left eyebrow, ruby necklace, white dhoti with red border, sword |
| arulmozhi | அருள்மொழி (later ராஜராஜன்), about 18 | calm, thoughtful, thin first moustache, neat topknot, tiger pendant, white dhoti with gold border |
| kundavai | குந்தவை, about 22 | steady eyes, side bun with jasmine, deep red silk with gold border |
| sundara | சுந்தர சோழன், king, about 50 | greying moustache, tall conical gold crown, jewelled collar, lion throne |
| uttama | உத்தம சோழன் (மதுராந்தகன்), about 38 | lean, unreadable, three ash stripes, rudraksha beads, pale shawl |
| brothers | சோமன், ரவிதாசன், பரமேஸ்வரன் | heavy-set eldest with grey tuft; tall gaunt middle one with thin moustache; broad stern youngest; shaved foreheads, sacred threads |
| virapandya | வீரபாண்டியன், Pandya king, about 45 | thick beard, fish-emblem crown, pearl necklaces, green shawl |

Rules from here on: kings, princes, the princess and the three brothers are shown with faces. Twentieth-century and living people (the scholars, Kalki) are still shown from behind or as hands only, because they are real and I will not invent their faces.
