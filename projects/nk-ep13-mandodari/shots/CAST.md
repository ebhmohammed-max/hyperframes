Rules
- Ravana's ten heads appear only as a shadow, a statue or a stone relief.
- Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Shiva are never shown by face: from behind, far away, as a silhouette, or as a hand or a foot.
- Hanuman is shown in full and always as a tall, mighty, crowned figure (never as a small monkey), on the channel owner's instruction of 17 Nov.
- Vibhishana, Akshakumara, Dhanyamalini, Kubera and the others have no fixed face; they are small or turned away.
- No blood or wounds. Present-day people from behind or hands only.
