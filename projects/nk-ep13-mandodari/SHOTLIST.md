# EP13 Mandodari: shot list

198 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: illustration of a story told in old texts, not history and not photographs. Ravana, Mandodari, Maya and Indrajit have invented faces, kept consistent with the reference pictures in shots/CAST.md (marked `refs`). Ravana is drawn with one head; the ten heads appear only as a shadow, a statue or a stone relief. Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Shiva are never shown by face: only from behind, far away, as a silhouette, or as a hand or a foot. Hanuman is shown in full as a mighty crowned figure, following the reference picture the channel owner supplied (cast key `hanuman`). No blood or wounds. Present-day people are seen from behind or as hands only. No readable writing (all text is set in the edit).

Looks: **L** Lanka palace interior; **G** Lanka, the golden city; **F** forest of the epic age; **H** hermitage and penance; **K** Mount Kailash; **A** the Ashoka grove; **W** the battlefield; **J** later tales (storybook); **M** manuscripts and poets; **T** North India today; **X** symbolic still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
