# EP13 Mandodari: fact sheet

Written 17 Nov 2026 with script v1. This episode retells what old texts say. It makes no claim that any of it is history, and the script says so in the hook.

**How far the checking went.** The sources below were read as search results and extracts, not as full texts. Verse numbers for Valmiki's Yuddha Kanda sarga 111 were seen in the text itself (valmikiramayan.net). Everything marked **check** should be read against the original before the final voice recording.

On-screen tags: **வால்மீகி ராமாயணம்** · **கம்ப ராமாயணம்** · **பிற்கால நூல்கள்** (later texts) · **ஊர் நம்பிக்கை** (local belief) · **ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து** (what scholars say).

Left out on purpose: the Shiva Tandava Stotra. It is not in Valmiki; it belongs to later tradition.

## 5. The branch stories

| Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|

## Tone

- Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Shiva are never shown by face in the pictures. Hanuman is shown in full as a mighty crowned figure, as the channel owner asked.
- The script calls Ravana's act wrong in Mandodari's own words, and does not judge any living tradition that honours him.
- "அதிர்ச்சியான" is used twice for the Sita-as-daughter story. If that feels too strong for the channel, change both to "ஆச்சரியமான".
