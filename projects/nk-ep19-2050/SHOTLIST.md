# EP19 2050-ல் உலகம் எப்படி இருக்கும்? (What will the world be like in 2050?): shot list

127 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration, none is a photograph or a record. Pictures of 2050 (look F) show a believable near future, not science fiction; they illustrate a projection and are not a prediction of how any real place will look. Nila, her grandmother and every other person are invented; no real, living or identifiable person is shown. The three film cliches of the hook (flying car, robot servant, house on Mars) have their own poster look (S) so that they read as fantasy. The 2019 Chennai water shortage and the floods are illustrated, not shown from news footage. No readable writing, no logos, no flags, no emblems: every number, name and source is set in the edit (film/fx.mjs). Lines that carry a full-screen graphic (the hundred dots) have no picture.

Looks: **N** today, Tamil Nadu and India; **F** the year 2050, a believable near future; **W** the world; **C** the Tamil Nadu coast; **D** symbolic still life; **P** the year 1990, like an old colour print; **S** a film cliche, painted as a fantasy; **O** setting given in the subject.
