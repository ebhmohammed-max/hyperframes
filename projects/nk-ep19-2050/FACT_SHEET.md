# EP19 — 2050-ல் உலகம் எப்படி இருக்கும்? Fact sheet

Version 1, 21 Nov 2026. Goes with `script/script.mjs` (69 lines).

**How to read this.** Nobody knows 2050. Every figure about it below is a **projection** by the body named, under the scenario named, and the film says it that way ("கணிப்பு", "கணக்கு", "போகலாம்", "வரை"). On-screen labels:
**U** ஐ.நா. கணிப்பு (a UN body's projection) · **R** ஆய்வு கணிப்பு (a published study, report or assessment) · **S** ஒரு பாதை, கணிப்பு இல்ல (a pathway that shows what would have to happen; not a forecast) · **F** பதிவான உண்மை (already happened, on record) · **O** கருத்து (our own reading or arithmetic) · **I** கற்பனை காட்சி (invented scene).

**Checked how.** Figures were taken from the publishers' own pages where the search returned them (UN, WHO, FAO, IPCC, IEA, World Bank, IHME) and otherwise from press reports of the named study. I could not open the full reports from here. Rows marked **(press)** rest on a press report only and should be checked against the original before the film is published.

## Invented (label I)

Nila, born tonight in Chennai, aged 24 in 2050, and her grandmother (55 now, 79 in 2050) are made up to give the numbers a face. Nothing about them is a fact. 2050 − 2026 = 24.

## The two "already decided" items: why the film may say so

1. **Ageing.** The people who will be old in 2050 are alive now. No policy changes how many people were born before 1990.
2. **About a hand span of sea-level rise by 2050.** IPCC: 10–25 cm more by 2050 in every emissions scenario.

Everything else in the film is said to be open, and for three of the gravest projections the source itself gives the size of the "but": migration (up to 80% lower with action), drug-resistant infections (92 million lives could be saved), energy (the IEA pathway).

## Kept out on purpose

- No claim that phones cause short sight; no claim that a named city "will be under water"; no date for the end of any job.
- Warming of 1.5°C in the early 2030s (IPCC AR6) was researched and left out to keep the last chapter on one idea.
- Dementia (153 million by 2050), heart disease and the Goldman Sachs 2075 ranking were researched and left out for length.
