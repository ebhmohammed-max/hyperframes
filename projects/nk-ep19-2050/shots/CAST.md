Rules
- Every picture is an illustration. None is a photograph or a record, including the 2019 water shortage and the floods.
- 2050 (look F) is a believable near future: today's city, denser, with solar roofs, electric buses, trees, a metro line. No flying vehicles, holograms, walking robots, neon, chrome or domes. These pictures illustrate a projection; they do not predict how a real place will look.
- The three film cliches of the hook (flying car, robot servant, house on Mars) are in the poster look S and are struck out on screen.
- Nila, her grandmother and all other people are invented. No real, living or identifiable person. Nila only from behind; children's faces soft or turned away.
- No readable writing, logos, flags or emblems. Numbers, names and sources are set in the edit (`film/fx.mjs`).
- Medical pictures stay calm: no wounds, no patients in distress; an empty bed stands for the deaths in N06 q5.
