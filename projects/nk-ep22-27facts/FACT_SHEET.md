# EP22 fact sheet: 27 உண்மைகள் (27 facts that make you question your existence)

Written 25 Nov 2026 with script v1. Every claim in the film carries one of five on-screen labels:

| Label | Tamil on screen | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| F | பதிவான உண்மை | measured or on the record (dates, distances, speeds) |
| E | அறிவியல் கணிப்பு | a published scientific estimate; the number can change |
| S | நிறுவப்பட்ட அறிவியல் | settled textbook science |
| C | கணக்கு | our arithmetic from published numbers |

**How far each source was read.** Facts 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 13, 15, 16, 17, 21, 23 and 26 rest on web search results read on 25 Nov 2026 (summaries of the papers and pages named below; no paper was opened in full). Facts 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 18, 19, 20, 22, 24 and 25 are standard textbook figures written from general knowledge and **not looked up in this session**; the source named is where to confirm them. Numbers are spoken in lakh and crore (1 lakh crore = 1 trillion).

## The 27 facts

## To check before publishing

1. **Facts not looked up in this session** (4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 18, 19, 20, 22, 24, 25): confirm each number against the source named. All are standard figures; none is expected to change.
2. **Fact 1, "more than half of you is not you":** true by cell count on the 2016 estimate (38 against 30), which the authors give with wide error bars. If you want it safer, say "about half".
3. **Fact 2:** "a whole body's worth" is a count, not a claim that every cell is replaced.
4. **Fact 5:** the 7 × 10^27 figure was not confirmed by the search; it is the usual estimate for a 70 kg adult.
5. **Fact 11:** "over 8 crore" uses the middle of each range (150 and 67 million years). The smallest possible gap is about 7.7 crore, still more than 6.6. The card reads "6.6 < 8.4".
6. **Fact 16:** the oldest shark evidence is fossil scales; the first complete shark fossils are younger.
7. **Fact 22:** "a day" means one turn on the axis (243 days). Sunrise to sunrise on Venus is about 117 Earth days, which is shorter than its year.
8. **Fact 23:** the stat card says "1,000+" galaxies; the voice says "thousands", as NASA does.
9. **Fact 26:** the voice says "November 2026" and "49 years" (launch 5 Sept 1977). If the film is published much later, the line still holds.
10. **Fact 27 and the hook** depend on the running time. Check them again against the final film length (see STATUS.md).
11. Pictures are illustrations, not telescope images or records. The Webb, Voyager and Moon pictures are drawn for this film.
