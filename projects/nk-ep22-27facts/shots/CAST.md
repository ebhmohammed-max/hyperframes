# EP22 picture rules

No reference pictures. The one recurring figure is held together by a fixed description in `shots/expand.py` (`REF`).

- Every picture is an illustration made for this film. None is a photograph, a telescope image or a record. The pictures show what a fact is about; they are not evidence for it.
- **No face of a real person.** The viewer (V), a young Tamil adult on a house terrace at night, is shown only from behind or as a silhouette. The Egyptian queen (fact 12) is seen from behind. Babies are shown as hands or wrapped bundles.
- Seven looks: **H** you, tonight (Tamil Nadu, lamp light and night sky); **B** inside the body (microscopic, teal and amber on dark); **T** deep time (prehistoric Earth, museum reconstruction); **E** Earth today (nature documentary); **C** space (navy, black, gold); **S** one object (still life); **O** setting given in the subject (Egypt, the Moon, 1977 launch, a ward).
- No readable writing, logos, flags or emblems; clocks have no numerals. Spacecraft carry no markings. Every number, name and source is set in the edit (`film/fx.mjs`).
- Real things drawn from description, not exact: the Great Pyramid, the Webb telescope, Voyager 1, the 1977 rocket, the Stegosaurus and T. rex.
