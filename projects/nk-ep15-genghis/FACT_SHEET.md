# EP15 Genghis Khan: fact sheet

Written 19 Nov 2026 with script v1. Topic in the user's words: "Genghis Khan: The Man Who Conquered the World". The film tests that title: he founded the empire; the "largest contiguous land empire" came under his sons and grandsons.

**How far the checking went.** Everything below was read as web-search results and short extracts, not as full pages or books. No primary text (the *Secret History*, Juvayni, Rashid al-Din, the *Yuan shi*) and no modern biography was read first-hand. Most of the other search hits were blogs and copies of Wikipedia, so agreement between them is weak evidence. Everything marked **check** should be read against a proper source before the final voice recording.

Sources, by letter:
- **W** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genghis_Khan
- **T** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_Mongol_conquests
- **G** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_descent_from_Genghis_Khan
- **P** popularmysteries.com, "The lost tomb of Genghis Khan" (a popular site; it cites UNESCO for the mountain)
- **I** unanswered.io and bharatdocs.com, on why he did not invade India (popular sites)
- **web** other search hits (blogs, mirrors) that were not kept by name

On-screen tags: **வரலாற்றுப் பதிவு** (record) · **மங்கோலியர் நூல்** (told by *The Secret History of the Mongols*, written after his death, part record and part legend) · **பிற்காலக் கதை** (later legend) · **ஆய்வாளர் கருத்து** (what researchers say) · **நிரூபிக்கப்படாத கூற்று** (unproven claim).

- The tale that molten silver was poured on Inalchuq ("according to one account").
- The doubt about Jochi's father.
