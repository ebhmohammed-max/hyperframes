# EP15 reference pictures (invented; none is a portrait of a real person)

Reference pictures go in `out/cast/` (stored in `<repo>/google_drive/ep15_pics/cast/`, Drive: `images/EP15_cast.zip`). Pass them as reference images so the faces stay the same.

No portrait of Genghis Khan was made in his lifetime; the best-known one was painted in 1278, about fifty years after his death. Every face below is therefore imagined, and the film says so in its last chapter.

| temujin | டெமுஜின் as a young man, about 25 (made from `khan`) | same face younger, thin moustache, no beard, bare head, dark blue deel, leather belt |

For ages between the references, say the age in the prompt ("now in his forties", "a youth of about fifteen", "now old and greyer").

Rules
- No blood, no bodies, nobody shown being hurt or killed. Wars are riders, dust, far smoke and empty streets.
- Other real people (Yesügei, Toghrul, Jebe, Subutai, Shah Muhammad, Inalchuq, Jalal al-Din, Kublai) from behind, far away, or with small unclear faces.
- Clergy of any faith from behind and with respect.
- No readable writing; no map that claims to be accurate (maps appear only as props without lettering).
- Present-day people from behind or as hands only.
- The famous 1278 portrait is never copied: it appears only as an out-of-focus album leaf in a museum case.
