# EP15 Genghis Khan: shot list

178 images. File names carry the running number, narration clip and line.

Rules: every picture is an illustration. No portrait of Genghis Khan was made in his lifetime, so his face in these pictures is invented (the film says so in its last chapter); it is kept alike with reference pictures (`temujin_boy`, `temujin`, `khan`). Börte, Hö'elün and Jamukha also have invented faces with reference pictures. Other real people (Yesügei, Toghrul, Jebe, Subutai, the Shah, the governor of Otrar, Jalal al-Din, Kublai) are shown from behind, far away or without a clear face. No blood, no bodies, nobody shown being hurt or killed: wars are shown as riders, dust, smoke far away and empty streets. Clergy of any faith are shown from behind and with respect. No readable writing and no maps that claim to be accurate. Present-day people from behind or as hands only.

Looks: **S** steppe life, late 12th century; **R** the khan and his army, early 13th century; **N** legend and funeral mood; **C** the cities of Khwarazm, about 1220; **J** northern China, about 1211 to 1215; **T** Mongolia today; **X** symbolic still life; **O** setting given in the subject.
