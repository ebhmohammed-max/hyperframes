# EP23 fact sheet

What each spoken claim rests on, chapter by chapter, and what to check before publishing. The sheet grows with the script.

**How the facts were gathered so far.** Three web searches (26 Nov 2026) confirmed the Academy figures, the shelter and men's-home dates, and the mother's death and Dr Bloch's words. Everything else in the hook and chapter 1 is written from general knowledge of the standard biographies (Kershaw, Ullrich, Hamann) and was **not looked up line by line**. Treat every row marked "memory" as unchecked.

## Points to check before publishing (so far)

1. Rows marked "memory" (30 of the 45 rows above).
2. Row 4: the number of children who died before him (the script gives none on purpose).
3. Row 11: the date of Edmund's death differs between books.
4. Row 18: he told the school-report story himself; the script softens what he did with it.
5. Row 30: the hour of death and the lit Christmas tree.
6. Row 34: "except one".
7. The hook's seven crore includes the war in Asia.
