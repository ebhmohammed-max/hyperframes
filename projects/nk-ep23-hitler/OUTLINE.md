# EP23 — ஹிட்லர்: வீடில்லாதவனில் இருந்து ராட்சசன் வரை

**Hitler: From Homeless to Monster.** Target running time: 1 hour 45 minutes (105 min). Tamil narration, voice `voice-02`.

## The question of the film

A homeless twenty-year-old stands in a shelter queue in Vienna in December 1909. Twenty-four years later he rules Germany; thirty years later he starts a war in which more than seven crore people die. He was not born a monster. Where did he change, who lifted him up, and how did an educated country follow him? The film answers in order, from 1889 to 1945, and keeps three threads in view all the way:

1. **The man**: failure, blame placed on others, a fixed belief in his own greatness, and one real gift, his voice.
2. **The helpers**: the people and institutions that thought they could use him (army officers, judges, press owners, conservative politicians, industrialists).
3. **The country**: defeat, humiliation, inflation, unemployment, and a public that accepted each step because the step before had already been accepted.

The film condemns what he did and shows how it became possible. It does not make him a hero, a genius or a mystery.

## Rules for the script

- Spoken Tamil, short sentences, the voice of the earlier episodes. Numbers are spoken as words; captions show digits.
- Every line carries an evidence label on screen: **பதிவான உண்மை** (documented), **சாட்சி சொன்னது** (a named witness said it later), **அவரே எழுதியது** (his own version in Mein Kampf, often not confirmed), **வரலாற்றாசிரியர் கருத்து** (historians' judgment or estimate), **கருத்து** (the film's comment).
- Where his own story and the records differ, the film says so.
- The main books behind the script: Ian Kershaw, *Hitler* (1998, 2000); Volker Ullrich, *Hitler* (2013, 2018); Brigitte Hamann, *Hitler's Vienna* (1996); Thomas Weber, *Hitler's First War* (2010); Richard J. Evans, the *Third Reich* trilogy (2003 to 2008); the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum and Yad Vashem for the numbers of the murdered. Each chapter's points are listed in `FACT_SHEET.md` with what to check before publishing.

## Rules for the pictures

See `shots/CAST.md`. In short: made film-still pictures only; Hitler's real face is shown at every age (user's instruction, 26 Nov 2026); 2.2:1 film frame; no flags, emblems, symbols or insignia; no readable writing; nothing graphic; nothing that celebrates him.

## How the film is made

The film is twenty times the length of EP22, and the limits per turn are the same (10 pictures, 10 narration clips, about 35 minutes of render). So it is built and delivered **chapter by chapter**:

1. Script of the chapter, narration (6 to 8 clips), timing.
2. Pictures: about 30 new pictures per chapter (the hook has 20). A picture stays on screen longer than in the short episodes and is shown as two shots (wide, then a closer crop); stat cards, date cards and quote cards carry the rest.
3. Render and upload: each chapter becomes one 1080p part on Drive as soon as it is finished. Narration and pictures go to Drive in the turn they are made.
4. At the end: the thirteen parts in order are the film. A 720p copy is cut into files under 100 MB (the Drive upload limit per file), about 25 minutes each.

About three turns per chapter, about forty turns for the whole film.
